MeetWise

Turn meetings into
actionable notes instantly

Upload a recording or transcript and MeetWise uses AI to generate accurate transcripts and structured meeting notes — with your privacy at the core.

AI Transcription

Self-hosted Whisper converts audio and video to timestamped text. Your recordings never touch a third-party cloud.

Smart Meeting Notes

Gemini AI structures your transcript into a summary, key decisions, action items, and participant list automatically.

Privacy First

All files are deleted after processing. PII is automatically masked. Notes stay encrypted in your own browser.

Record in Browser

Start a live audio or video recording directly from the dashboard — no extra software needed.

Flexible Export

Download your transcript and notes as PDF or TXT with one click. Your data, your format.

Secure by Default

Two-factor authentication, bcrypt password hashing, and AES-256 encrypted local storage protect your account.
